What the design covers

A full building project touches six disciplines. The brief scopes each one and says who owns it.

Survey & Platting

Boundary and topographic survey, easement research, and platting support to establish legal lots and dedications.

Site & Landscape

Site layout, parking and access, grading concept, tree preservation, and a landscape plan that fits the ordinance.

Civil

Drainage and detention design, water / wastewater / storm routing, and the studies the city will ask for.

Architectural

Floor plans, elevations, sections, and a code analysis locking occupancy, construction type, and egress.

Structural

Foundation and superstructure design, sealed — coordinated with the architectural set from concept on.

MEP

Mechanical, electrical, and plumbing design, sealed, with energy-code compliance carried through.
