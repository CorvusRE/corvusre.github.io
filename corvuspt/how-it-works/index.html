How it works

AI checks what humans usually miss.

County records, comps, land value, improvement value, site issues, zoning, income, prior‑year values, BPP assets, depreciation, deadlines, hearings, tax bills, payments, refunds, and final savings — all connected through one property record.

  1. 1

    Add Your Property

    Enter the property address or upload your Texas appraisal notice. We'll use it to create your property record and start the review.

  2. 2

    Verify the Official CAD Record

    Corvus AI identifies the correct appraisal district, matches your property, and verifies the official assessment and property details.

  3. 3

    Corvus AI Analyzes Your Property

    AI reviews comparable properties, land and improvement values, income, site factors, prior assessments, and other available evidence to identify potential protest opportunities.

  4. 4

    Review Your Protest Strategy

    See whether a protest is recommended, why, the estimated savings, supporting evidence, and the next steps — all explained in plain English.

  5. 5

    You File — or Our Team Does

    With Owner-Managed CorvusPT, you file, attend the hearing and talk to the county, using the evidence and step-by-step instructions the AI prepares. With Corvus-Managed, CorvusPT's team files, talks to the county and represents you — and nothing is settled without your approval.

  6. 6

    Track Results, Payments & Savings

    Follow your protest status, final value, tax bills, payments, refunds, and annual savings from one property dashboard.
