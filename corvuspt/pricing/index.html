12-Month Subscription Commitment

All subscriptions require a mandatory 12-month commitment. You may choose to pay the full subscription fee upfront or in 12 monthly installments.

By selecting monthly payments, you agree to the full 12-month subscription term. Your saved credit card will be automatically charged each month for 12 consecutive months. Monthly payments are an installment option and do not constitute a month-to-month subscription.

Please note: Early cancellation is not permitted, and the full 12-month subscription commitment remains payable regardless of the selected payment frequency.

How your plan works
  • Monthly: your plan runs for 12 months to start. Once 13 months are complete, you can cancel with 60 days' notice. If you stay, your next 12 months begin in month 15.
  • Yearly: one payment covers 12 months, at half price for your first year. It renews each year, and you can turn renewal off at any time.

Savings Protection, included with both plans. If CorvusPT doesn't find any savings for you in 2027, your unused value carries forward and your next year of protest support is free.
